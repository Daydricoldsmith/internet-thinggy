Who we are

Drowning Violets is a four-piece from Portland blending shoegaze haze with post-punk grit. Vocalist Mira Laurent leads shimmering guitars, tidal synths, and a rhythm section that never lets you touch the ground.

The band formed in 2019 and quickly became known for immersive live shows and cinematic soundscapes. Influences range from Slowdive and Cocteau Twins to modern torchbearers like Japanese Breakfast.
